ZSpace Disk 隐私政策 Privacy Policy for ZSpace Disk

ZSpace Disk 只会扫描你在 Mac 上选择的磁盘和文件夹。它不创建账户,不连接服务器,也不收集、传输或出售任何数据。 ZSpace Disk scans disks and folders you choose, on your Mac. It does not create an account, does not connect to a server, and does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

文件名、大小以及你授权的文件夹都只留在本机。应用会记住你已授权的文件夹,以便再次扫描。你可以在系统设置中撤销该权限。 File names, sizes, and the folders you allow stay on your device. The app remembers folders you have allowed so it can scan them again. You can revoke that access in System Settings.
