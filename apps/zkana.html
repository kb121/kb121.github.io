ZKana 隐私政策 Privacy Policy for ZKana

ZKana 是一个平假名记忆练习应用。它只记录你的练习数据:每个假名出现、答对、答错的次数,以及你的练习设置。这些数据保存在你的设备上。 ZKana is a hiragana practice app. The only data it keeps is your practice record: how often each kana was shown, answered right or wrong, and your practice settings. This data is stored on your device.

如果你在设备上登录了 iCloud,这些练习数据会通过 iCloud 键值存储同步到你自己 Apple 账户下的其他设备(Mac、iPad、iPhone、Apple Watch)。同步由 Apple 的 iCloud 完成,数据只存在于你的 iCloud 账户中,我无法访问。你可以在系统设置里关闭本应用的 iCloud 访问,或在应用的“设置”里重置统计,重置会同步清除所有设备上的记录。 If you are signed in to iCloud on the device, this practice data is synced through iCloud Key-Value Storage to your other devices under the same Apple account (Mac, iPad, iPhone, Apple Watch). The sync is performed by Apple's iCloud; the data lives only in your own iCloud account and I cannot access it. You can turn off iCloud for the app in System Settings, or reset the statistics in the app's Settings, which clears the record on every device.

应用不创建账户,不运行服务器,不含广告或第三方 SDK,不收集、传输或出售任何数据。可选的朗读功能使用设备自带的日语语音,在本机完成。 The app does not create an account, does not run a server, contains no ads or third-party SDKs, and does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. The optional read-aloud feature uses the Japanese voice built into your device and runs on the device.
