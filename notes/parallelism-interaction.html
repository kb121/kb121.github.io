卡间怎么对话:五种并行的交互编排How GPUs Talk: The Choreography of Five Parallelism Schemes

上一篇通信量账本算的是传多少字节。 这篇换个视角,只看编排:谁跟谁通信、在前向反向的哪个时刻通信、通信的时候计算是停着还是照跑。 两张卡传 1 GiB,和八张卡各传 128 MiB 却要全体对齐 —— 字节数一样,对性能的影响完全是两回事。The previous note, the communication ledger, counted how many bytes move. This one changes the angle and looks only at choreography: who talks to whom, at which moment of the forward and backward pass, and whether computation stops while they talk. Two GPUs moving 1 GiB, versus eight GPUs each moving 128 MiB but all having to align — the same byte count, and completely different consequences for performance.

一、先分清三种交互形态1 · Three interaction shapes

五种并行的通信看起来五花八门,但拓扑上只有三类。认出是哪一类,就知道它能不能跨节点、卡多了会不会崩。The communication of five parallelism strategies looks varied, but topologically there are only three kinds. Recognise which kind, and you know whether it can cross nodes and whether it collapses as GPUs are added.

动图 1:三种交互拓扑Figure 1: three interaction topologies
① 副本组内规约① Reduce within a replica group
DP · ZeRO

每张卡算的是不同数据、相同模型,最后把梯度对齐。全组参与,但一个 step 只做一次,而且能和反向重叠。Each GPU computes different data on the same model and aligns the gradients at the end. The whole group participates, but only once per step, and it can overlap with the backward pass.

② 全组交换② All-to-all exchange
TP · EP · Ulysses

每张卡都要和组里所有卡换数据。全连接、高频、强同步 —— 谁慢一点全组都得等。卡数一多就吃不消。Every GPU exchanges data with every other GPU in the group. Fully connected, frequent, tightly synchronised — if one lags, the whole group waits. It does not hold up as GPU count rises.

③ 邻居传递③ Neighbour hand-off
PP · Ring-Attention

只跟左右邻居说话,点对点,不需要全局同步。天然适合跨节点,也最容易和计算重叠。Talks only to the neighbours on either side, point to point, with no global synchronisation. Naturally suited to crossing nodes, and the easiest to overlap with computation.

一句话记法:① 一步一次,② 层层都要,③ 只找隔壁。 后面每种策略的性格,基本都能由它属于哪一类推出来。One line to remember: ① once per step, ② every single layer, ③ next door only. Each strategy's temperament below follows more or less directly from which of the three it is.

二、DP:各算各的,最后对一次账2 · DP: everyone works alone, then settles up once

DP 是交互最"稀疏"的一种:整个前向反向期间各卡互不打扰,只在反向结束时把梯度对齐一次。 ring all-reduce 分两个半程 —— 先 Reduce-Scatter 绕一圈让每卡持有一段的最终和,再 All-Gather 绕一圈把结果散开。DP has the sparsest interaction of the five: throughout the forward and backward pass the GPUs leave each other alone, aligning gradients exactly once when the backward finishes. A ring all-reduce runs in two halves — Reduce-Scatter goes round once so each GPU holds the final sum for one segment, then All-Gather goes round again to spread the results.

两个半程各绕 N−1 步,所以 All-Reduce 的每卡收发是 2·(N−1)/N·X。 实际框架会把梯度分桶:某一层反向一算完就立刻发它的桶,不等整个反向结束 —— 于是这一大坨通信被藏在了反向计算背后,这是 DP 能扩到几千卡的关键。Each half takes N−1 steps, so an All-Reduce sends and receives 2·(N−1)/N·X per GPU. Real frameworks bucket the gradients: as soon as one layer's backward finishes, its bucket goes out without waiting for the rest — so this large lump of communication disappears behind the backward computation. That is what lets DP scale to thousands of GPUs.

三、TP:每层两次会合,谁都不能先走3 · TP: two rendezvous per layer, and nobody leaves early

TP 是另一个极端。每张卡只持有权重的一片,算出来的是部分和,必须拼起来才有意义。 于是每层要停两次:attention 的 o_proj 之后一次,FFN 的 down_proj 之后一次。TP is the other extreme. Each GPU holds one shard of the weights and computes a partial sum, which is meaningless until assembled. So every layer stops twice: once after attention's o_proj, once after the FFN's down_proj.

关键不在字节数,在阻塞:All-Reduce 的结果是下一步计算的输入,没法拿计算去盖住它。 而且它是集合通信,整组以最慢的那张卡为准。斜纹区就是快卡在干等。 320 个硬同步点摊在一次前向反向里,这就是 TP 必须待在 NVLink 域内的真正原因。What matters is not the byte count but the blocking: the All-Reduce result is the input to the next step, so no computation can hide it. And being a collective, the whole group runs at the pace of its slowest GPU. The hatched region is the fast ones idling. 320 hard synchronisation points spread across one forward-backward pass is the real reason TP has to stay inside the NVLink domain.

四、PP:只跟左右邻居说话4 · PP: talking only to the neighbours

PP 的交互最简单 —— send 给下一段,recv 上一段的,仅此而已。 没有集合通信,没有全局同步。代价换到了另一个地方:排队。PP has the simplest interaction — send to the next stage, recv from the previous one, and that is it. No collectives, no global synchronisation. The price is paid elsewhere: queuing.

五、EP:谁跟谁通信是动态的5 · EP: who talks to whom is decided at runtime

前面四种的通信对象在开跑前就定死了。EP 不一样:token 去哪张卡,由 router 当场决定。 这带来了两个别处没有的麻烦。In the previous four, communication partners are fixed before the run starts. EP is different: which GPU a token goes to is decided on the spot by the router. That creates two problems the others do not have.

第一个麻烦是形状不确定:每张卡收到多少 token 事先不知道,缓冲要按最坏情况开。 第二个是长尾:All-to-All 要等最慢的一对完成,只要有一张卡收到的 token 明显偏多, 整组就被它拖住。拖动倾斜度能看到等待时间怎么被放大 —— 这就是 EPLB 把热门专家复制到多卡的理由。The first problem is unknown shapes: how many tokens a GPU will receive is not known in advance, so buffers must be sized for the worst case. The second is the tail: an All-to-All waits for the slowest pair to finish, so a single GPU receiving noticeably more tokens holds up the whole group. 六、SP / CP:三种切序列,三种交互6 · SP / CP: three ways to shard a sequence, three interaction styles

切序列这条轴上有三种做法,名字容易混,但交互方式完全不同。 机制细节见序列并行方案对比,这里只看卡间怎么动。There are three approaches on the sequence-sharding axis. The names are easy to confuse, but the interaction patterns are entirely different. For the mechanisms see comparing the sequence-parallel schemes; here we only look at what moves between GPUs.

动图 6:三种切序列方式的卡间动作(点标签切换)Figure 6: what moves between GPUs in each of the three (click a label to switch)
最值得注意的是第三种:Ring 是唯一能把通信藏进计算里的。 收到上一块 K/V 的同时就开始算本地那部分注意力,下一块在后台传 —— 通信量虽然不随 N 下降, 但只要单块计算时间盖得住单跳传输,墙钟时间就几乎不涨。前两种做不到:它们的通信结果是下一步的输入。The third one deserves the most attention: Ring is the only one that can hide communication inside computation. As one K/V block arrives, the local part of the attention starts computing while the next block transfers in the background — the communication volume does not fall with N, but as long as one block's computation covers one hop's transfer, wall-clock time barely moves. The first two cannot do this: their communication result is the next step's input.

八、组合起来:交互的层次8 · Combining them: layers of interaction

实际系统是几种叠在一起的。排布原则可以直接从上面的性格推出来: 不能重叠、又是全连接的往里放;能重叠、只找邻居的往外放。A real system stacks several. The placement principle follows directly from the temperaments above: put the non-overlappable, fully connected ones innermost; put the overlappable, neighbours-only ones outermost.

九、要点9 · Takeaways

01
拓扑只有三类There are only three topologies

副本组规约(DP)、全组交换(TP / EP / Ulysses)、邻居传递(PP / Ring)。认出属于哪一类,就能推出它能不能跨节点、卡多了会不会崩。Replica-group reduce (DP), all-to-all exchange (TP / EP / Ulysses), neighbour hand-off (PP / Ring). Identify which one applies and you can predict whether it crosses nodes and whether it collapses at scale.

02
能否重叠比传多少更要命Overlappability matters more than volume

DP 每 step 传上 TB 却能扩到几千卡,TP 每次几十 MiB 却出机柜就崩。区别是 DP 能藏在反向后面,而 TP 的结果就是下一步的输入,藏无可藏。DP moves terabytes per step and scales to thousands of GPUs; TP moves tens of MiB and falls apart outside the rack. The difference is that DP hides behind the backward pass while TP's result is the next step's input, with nowhere to hide.

03
集合通信按最慢的那张卡计A collective runs at the pace of its slowest GPU

TP 和 EP 都是全组同步,快卡只能干等。所以真正的敌人往往不是带宽,是抖动和不均衡 —— EP 尤其明显。TP and EP both synchronise the whole group, and the fast GPUs simply wait. So the real enemy is often not bandwidth but jitter and imbalance — most visibly in EP.

04
PP 的气泡只能靠 M 救Only M can save PP's bubble

(N−1)/(M+N−1),分母涨得比分子快。加 micro-batch 有效,减 stage 反而可能放不下模型。动图 4 里可以自己拖着验证。(N−1)/(M+N−1): the denominator grows faster than the numerator. Adding micro-batches works; removing stages may leave the model unable to fit. Drag it in Figure 4 and check.

05
EP 的通信对象是运行时才知道的EP's communication partners are only known at runtime

其他四种在开跑前就定死了收发关系,EP 由 router 当场决定。于是缓冲要按最坏情况开,长尾还会拖住整组 —— 这两件事都是别处没有的。The other four fix their send/receive relationships before the run; EP has the router decide on the spot. So buffers must be sized for the worst case and the tail holds up the whole group — neither problem exists elsewhere.

06
Ring 用步数换重叠Ring trades hop count for overlap

它绕 N−1 跳,总量不随 N 下降,看账本是亏的。但它是切序列里唯一能边收边算的,只要计算盖得住单跳,墙钟时间几乎不涨。It goes N−1 hops and its total does not fall with N, so on the ledger it looks like a loss. But it is the only sequence-sharding scheme that computes as it receives, and as long as computation covers one hop, wall-clock time barely moves.
