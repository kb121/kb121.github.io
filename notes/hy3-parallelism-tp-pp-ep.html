从一份 config 读懂并行切分:TP=4 / PP=4 / EP=4Reading Parallelism off a config.json: TP=4 / PP=4 / EP=4

并行策略这件事,光看名词很容易糊涂:TP 到底切的是什么?切 head_dim 还是切 head 数?EP 和 TP 是不是一回事? 这篇笔记不讲抽象定义,直接拿一份真实的 tencent/Hy3 config.json 当底稿,把 TP=4、PP=4、EP=4 落到每一个张量的 shape 上,配动图看清数据怎么流。最后在 Extra 里补齐 DP / SP / CP 等其余维度。Parallelism is easy to muddle when you only have the vocabulary: what does TP actually shard — head_dim, or the number of heads? Are EP and TP the same thing? Rather than abstract definitions, this note takes a real tencent/Hy3 config.json and works TP=4, PP=4, EP=4 down to the shape of every tensor, with animations for how the data moves. An Extra section covers the remaining dimensions — DP, SP, CP and the rest.

一句话总览: 三种并行切的是三个完全正交的维度——TP 切"宽度"(一层之内的 head 数 / FFN 中间维),PP 切"深度"(80 层分成 4 段),EP 切"专家数"(192 个专家分给 4 组)。而 head_dim=128 是唯一那个谁都不许切的维度。In one line: the three shard three fully orthogonal dimensions. TP shards width (head count and FFN intermediate size within a layer), PP shards depth (80 layers into 4 segments), and EP shards expert count (192 experts across 4 groups). head_dim=128 is the one dimension nobody is allowed to cut.

1 · 第一个陷阱:head_dim ≠ hidden_size / num_heads1 · First trap: head_dim ≠ hidden_size / num_heads

绝大多数人读 config 会下意识地算 4096 / 64 = 64,认为 head_dim=64。但这份 config 显式写了 head_dim: 128。两者对不上:Most people reading this config reflexively compute 4096 / 64 = 64 and assume head_dim=64. But the config states head_dim: 128 explicitly, and the two do not agree:

为什么这个细节对并行很关键? 因为切分的分母是 num_heads,而张量的宽度是 num_heads × head_dim。如果你按 hidden_size/TP 去算 q_proj 的分片大小,会得到 1024,而正确答案是 8192/4 = 2048——差了整整一倍,shape 直接对不上。Why does this detail matter for parallelism? Because the divisor for sharding is num_heads while the tensor width is num_heads × head_dim. Size q_proj's shard as hidden_size/TP and you get 1024, when the right answer is 8192/4 = 2048 — off by a factor of two, and the shapes simply will not line up.

2 · TP=4:切的是 head 的个数,不是 head_dim2 · TP=4 shards the number of heads, not head_dim

这是本文最想讲清的一点。张量并行在 attention 上的切法是:把 64 个 head 发牌一样分给 4 张卡,每卡 16 个完整的 head。每个 head 内部的 head_dim=128 原封不动。This is the point the note most wants to land. Tensor parallelism shards attention by dealing the 64 heads out to 4 GPUs like cards, 16 complete heads each. The head_dim=128 inside each head is left untouched.

为什么 head_dim 切不得?Why can head_dim not be sharded?

因为一个 head 内部是一条不可分割的规约链:QKT 要沿 head_dim 做点积,softmax 又要沿序列做归一化。若把 head_dim 切开,每算一个注意力分数都得跨卡 all-reduce:Because a head contains an indivisible reduction chain: QKT takes a dot product along head_dim, and softmax normalises along the sequence. Split head_dim and every single attention score needs a cross-GPU all-reduce:

✅ 按 head 切(TP 的做法)✅ Shard by head (what TP does)
Q[16h,128]×KT →softmax→×V

整条链全在本卡完成,16 个 head 各算各的。只在最后 o_proj 之后做一次 all-reduce 把 4 张卡的部分和加起来。通信 = 每层 1 次。The whole chain completes locally, with 16 heads computed independently. Only after o_proj is there one all-reduce to sum the partials across 4 GPUs. Communication: once per layer.

❌ 按 head_dim 切(没人这么干)❌ Shard by head_dim (nobody does this)
Q[64h,32]×KT ⇅ all-reduce→softmax

点积被劈成 4 段部分和,每个 token、每个 head 的每个分数都要跨卡求和才能进 softmax。通信量爆炸,而且 QK-norm(qk_norm: true)也要沿 head_dim 归一化,同样被劈开。The dot product breaks into 4 partial sums, so every score of every head of every token needs a cross-GPU sum before softmax. Communication explodes — and QK-norm (qk_norm: true) also normalises along head_dim, so it gets split too.

GQA 的自洽性检查: 64 个 Q head 配 8 个 KV head,组大小 = 8。TP=4 后每卡是 16 Q + 2 KV,组大小还是 16/2 = 8 ✅。这说明 TP 必须整除 num_key_value_heads——本模型 KV head 只有 8,所以 TP ∈ {1,2,4,8} 是干净的;TP=16 就会不够分,只能把 KV head 复制到多卡(浪费显存)或改用别的策略。MoE 大模型 TP 开不大,这是主因之一。A GQA consistency check: 64 Q heads to 8 KV heads means a group size of 8. After TP=4 each GPU has 16 Q + 2 KV, so the group size is still 16/2 = 8 ✅. This means TP must divide num_key_value_heads — with only 8 KV heads here, TP ∈ {1,2,4,8} is clean, while TP=16 runs short and forces you to replicate KV heads across GPUs (wasting memory) or switch strategy. This is one of the main reasons TP cannot be pushed far on large MoE models.

3 · PP=4:切的是"深度"3 · PP=4 shards depth

流水线并行不动任何一个张量的 shape,它只是把 80 层按顺序切成 4 段,每段 20 层放一张(组)卡上。注意 first_k_dense_replace: 1 让第 0 层是 dense FFN,其余 79 层才是 MoE:Pipeline parallelism changes no tensor shape at all; it just cuts the 80 layers into 4 sequential segments of 20 layers, one per GPU (group). Note that first_k_dense_replace: 1 makes layer 0 a dense FFN, leaving 79 MoE layers:

PP 的代价不是通信,而是 bubble(气泡):stage 3 必须等 stage 0/1/2 都算完才能开工。解法是把 batch 拆成 micro-batch 流水起来:PP's cost is not communication but bubbles: stage 3 cannot start until stages 0, 1 and 2 are done. The fix is splitting the batch into micro-batches and pipelining them:

本模型 PP 切分的隐藏坑:负载不均。 Stage 0 多背一个 embedding(120832×4096 ≈ 0.49B),Stage 3 多背 lm_head(又 0.49B)加上 num_nextn_predict_layers: 1 的 MTP 层。所以"每段 20 层"看似整齐,实际首尾两段更重。生产上常用不均匀切分(如 21/20/20/19)把首尾的额外负担摊平。The hidden trap in this model's PP split: load imbalance. Stage 0 additionally carries the embedding (120832×4096 ≈ 0.49B), and stage 3 carries lm_head (another 0.49B) plus the MTP layer from num_nextn_predict_layers: 1. So "20 layers each" looks tidy but the first and last stages are heavier. Production setups often use an uneven split such as 21/20/20/19 to spread that extra weight.

4 · EP=4:切的是"专家数"4 · EP=4 shards the expert count

MoE 层里 192 个专家总共 3.62B 参数,但每个 token 只激活 8 个。专家并行的思路是:与其把每个专家都切碎(TP),不如让每张卡各自拿着一整批完整的专家。 192 / 4 = 每卡 48 个完整专家。A MoE layer's 192 experts total 3.62B parameters, but each token activates only 8. Expert parallelism's idea: rather than shredding every expert (TP), let each GPU hold a whole batch of complete experts. 192 / 4 = 48 complete experts per GPU.

EP 的优点What EP buys you

  • 专家权重完整不切,GEMM 形状规整(1536 不用再除以 4),kernel 效率高。Expert weights stay whole, so GEMM shapes stay regular (1536 is not divided by 4) and kernels run efficiently.
  • 显存线性下降:每卡只存 48 个专家。Memory falls linearly: each GPU stores only 48 experts.
  • 通信量 ∝ 激活的 token 数,与专家总数无关——192 个专家和 1920 个专家通信一样多。Communication scales with the number of active tokens, not the expert count — 192 experts and 1920 experts cost the same.

EP 的代价What EP costs you

  • 两次 All-to-All(dispatch + combine),延迟敏感、对网络拓扑挑剔。Two All-to-Alls (dispatch and combine): latency-sensitive and fussy about network topology.
  • 负载不均:热门专家所在卡被打爆,其余卡空等(见 Extra 的 EPLB)。Load imbalance: the GPU holding a popular expert gets hammered while the others wait (see EPLB in the Extra).
  • 需要 capacity factor / 丢 token 策略,或做 padding 浪费算力。Needs a capacity factor or a token-dropping policy, or you pad and waste compute.
共享专家怎么处理? num_shared_experts: 1 意味着有 1 个专家每个 token 都要过。它不参与 EP 切分(切了等于所有 token 都得跨卡),通常是在每张卡上按 TP 切分并复制一份——即每卡持有 1536/4 = 384 的中间维分片。这样它和 attention 一样走 all-reduce,不进 All-to-All。What about the shared expert? num_shared_experts: 1 means one expert every token must pass through. It stays out of the EP split (sharding it would send every token across GPUs) and is usually TP-sharded and replicated on every GPU — each holding a 1536/4 = 384 slice of the intermediate dimension. That way it goes through all-reduce like attention, never All-to-All.

5 · 三者合体:16 张卡怎么摆5 · All three together: laying out 16 GPUs

TP=4, PP=4, EP=4 最常见的落法(也是 vLLM --tensor-parallel-size 4 --pipeline-parallel-size 4 --enable-expert-parallel 的语义)是:EP 复用 TP 那一组卡。世界大小 = TP × PP = 16 张卡,而不是 4×4×4=64。The usual way to land TP=4, PP=4, EP=4 — and the semantics of vLLM's --tensor-parallel-size 4 --pipeline-parallel-size 4 --enable-expert-parallel — is for EP to reuse the same GPUs as TP. World size = TP × PP = 16 GPUs, not 4×4×4 = 64.

EP 不是独立乘上去的一维。 vLLM 里 enable_expert_parallel 的注释写得很直白——"Use expert parallelism instead of tensor parallelism for MoE layers"。也就是说同一批卡:attention 层按 TP 切,MoE 层改按 EP 切,EP size = TP × DP。所以别把 4×4×4 当成 64 卡去规划机器。EP is not an independent multiplicative dimension. vLLM's comment on enable_expert_parallel says it plainly: "Use expert parallelism instead of tensor parallelism for MoE layers". Same GPUs: attention layers shard by TP, MoE layers shard by EP instead, with EP size = TP × DP. So do not plan hardware as if 4×4×4 meant 64 GPUs.
