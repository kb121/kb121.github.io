并行策略的通信量账本The Communication Bill of Parallelism

选并行策略最后都会归到一个问题:这么切,每张卡每步要多传多少字节? 这篇把 DP / TP / PP / EP / CP 放进同一套符号里各算一遍,给出与 B、S、H、L 和并行度的比例关系, 再横向比,最后落到两个实际问题:怎么排布,以及最少要几张卡。Choosing a parallelism strategy always comes down to one question: with this sharding, how many bytes does each GPU move per step? This note puts DP / TP / PP / EP / CP into one notation and works each one out, giving its proportionality to B, S, H, L and the degree of parallelism, then compares them side by side, and finally lands on two practical questions: how to lay them out, and the minimum number of GPUs.

前置两篇:TP / PP / EP 怎么切到张量 shape 上讲的是切法, 主流序列并行方案对比讲的是 SP 各方案的机制与原语。 这篇不重复机制,只算账。Two prerequisites: how TP / PP / EP shard down to tensor shapes covers the sharding itself, and comparing the sequence-parallel schemes covers the mechanisms and primitives of SP. This note does not repeat mechanism; it does the arithmetic.

四、DP:通信量只跟参数量有关4 · DP: volume depends only on parameter count

DP 每卡一份完整模型,算完各自的梯度后做一次 All-Reduce。关键特征是: 它的通信量与 S、B 完全无关,只跟参数量走,而且每个 step 才一次。Every DP rank holds a full model and does one All-Reduce after computing its own gradients. The defining characteristic: its volume is entirely independent of S and B, tracking only the parameter count, and it happens once per step.

每卡每 stepPer GPU per step 朴素 DP = 2·(N−1)/N · Pg · b ≈ 2 Pg b
ZeRO-3 = 前向 AllGather + 反向 AllGather + 梯度 ReduceScatter ≈ 3 Pg bnaive DP = 2·(N−1)/N · Pg · b ≈ 2 Pg b
ZeRO-3 = fwd AllGather + bwd AllGather + gradient ReduceScatter ≈ 3 Pg b

Pg 是 DP 组内每份副本的参数量,即 P / (TP·PP·EP) —— 如果同时开了 TP / PP / EP,参数已经被它们切过,DP 只在剩下的那份上同步。Pg is the parameter count of one replica inside the DP group, that is P / (TP·PP·EP) — if TP / PP / EP are also enabled, the parameters have already been sharded by them and DP only synchronises what is left.

这里有个很容易搞错的点:分母里没有 DP 度。 ZeRO-3 把参数分片存放,但 All-Gather 要把它们拼回完整的一份才能算, 所以每卡收发量 ≈ 完整参数量,加 DP 卡并不会让每卡的通信量下降,只是把 batch 摊薄了。 ZeRO-3 比朴素 DP 多 1.5 倍通信,换来参数、梯度、优化器状态全部分片 —— 对 295B 这个量级这是唯一可行的选择,光 AdamW 的状态就是权重的 7 倍。Here is the part people get wrong most often: there is no DP degree in the denominator. ZeRO-3 shards parameter storage, but All-Gather has to reassemble a full copy before computing, so per-GPU traffic ≈ the full parameter count, and adding DP ranks does not reduce per-GPU communication — it only thins out the batch. ZeRO-3 costs 1.5× the communication of naive DP in exchange for sharding parameters, gradients and optimizer state — at the 295B scale it is the only viable option, since AdamW state alone is 7× the weights.

五、TP:每层两次全归约,与序列成正比5 · TP: two all-reduces per layer, proportional to sequence length

TP 把每个算子内部切开,代价是每层都要把结果拼回来:attention 的 o_proj 之后一次 All-Reduce, FFN 的 down_proj 之后一次。反向再来两次。TP splits the interior of each operator, at the cost of having to reassemble the result at every layer: one All-Reduce after attention's o_proj, one after the FFN's down_proj. Two more on the backward.

每卡每 micro-batch(整模型)Per GPU per micro-batch (whole model) TP = 4 次 × 2·(N−1)/N · A × L ≈ 8 · L · B·S·H·b
TP = 4 × 2·(N−1)/N · A × L ≈ 8 · L · B·S·H·b
 L=80, A=32 MiB, N=8 → 约 17.5 GiBL=80, A=32 MiB, N=8 → about 17.5 GiB

17.5 GiB。这是所有策略里最大的一档,而且注意分子里有 S —— 序列翻倍,通信量就翻倍。 更要命的是频率:每层 4 次,80 层就是 320 次集合通信,每一次都是同步点。 这就是TP 必须压在 NVLink 域内的原因,跨节点做 TP 基本等于自杀。17.5 GiB. The largest of any strategy — and note the S in the numerator: double the sequence and you double the volume. Worse is the frequency: 4 per layer, so 320 collectives across 80 layers, every one of them a synchronisation point. This is why TP has to stay inside the NVLink domain; running TP across nodes is close to suicide.

Megatron-SP 是免费搭档。它把 TP 的 All-Reduce 拆成 Reduce-Scatter + All-Gather —— 2X = X + X,总量一个字节都没变,但 LayerNorm / Dropout 那段激活也跟着被切成 N 份。 白拿的显存,没有不开的理由。Megatron-SP is a free companion. It splits TP's All-Reduce into Reduce-Scatter + All-Gather — 2X = X + X, so the total does not change by a single byte — while the LayerNorm and Dropout activations get sharded N ways too. Free memory; there is no reason not to enable it.

六、PP:通信最省,代价是气泡6 · PP: the cheapest communication, paid for in bubbles

PP 只在 stage 边界传激活,层内什么都不用传。这让它成为通信量最小的策略,小两个数量级。PP only passes activations at stage boundaries and sends nothing within a layer. That makes it the cheapest strategy by two orders of magnitude.

每个 stage 边界、每个 micro-batchPer stage boundary, per micro-batch PP = 前向 A + 反向 A = 2 · B·S·H·b = 64 MiB
PP = fwd A + bwd A = 2 · B·S·H·b = 64 MiB
 对比 TP 的 17.5 GiB,差约 280 倍Against TP's 17.5 GiB, a factor of about 280

而且是 P2P —— 只跟相邻 stage 说话,不是集合通信,天然可以跨节点走 IB。 PP 的代价不在带宽,在气泡:And it is P2P — talking only to the adjacent stage, not a collective, so it naturally crosses nodes over IB. PP's cost is not bandwidth but bubbles:

流水线气泡占比Pipeline bubble fraction bubble = (Npp − 1) / (M + Npp − 1)
M = micro-batch 数。M=8, N=4 → 27%;M=32, N=4 → 8.6%M = micro-batch count. M=8, N=4 → 27%; M=32, N=4 → 8.6%

所以 PP 的经验法则是 M ≥ 4·Npp。 调度上 GPipe → 1F1B → interleaved 1F1B → zero-bubble 这条演进线,做的都是同一件事:把这个分数榨小。Hence PP's rule of thumb, M ≥ 4·Npp. The scheduling lineage GPipe → 1F1B → interleaved 1F1B → zero-bubble is all doing one thing: squeezing that fraction down.

七、EP:All-to-All,被 top-k 放大7 · EP: All-to-All, amplified by top-k

EP 把专家分到不同卡上,于是每个 token 要被送到选中专家所在的卡(dispatch), 算完再收回来(combine)。前向两次 All-to-All,反向两次。EP spreads experts across GPUs, so every token must be sent to the GPU holding its chosen expert (dispatch) and brought back after computing (combine). Two All-to-Alls forward, two backward.

每卡每 micro-batch(全部 MoE 层)Per GPU per micro-batch (all MoE layers) EP = 4 次 × (N−1)/N · min(k, N) · A × Lmoe
EP = 4 × (N−1)/N · min(k, N) · A × Lmoe
 k=8, N=8, L=80 → 约 70 GiBk=8, N=8, L=80 → about 70 GiB

min(k, N) 那一项是关键:一个 token 选了 k 个专家,但如果其中几个恰好落在同一张卡上, 只需要发一次。所以放大倍数是 k 和 Nep 里的较小者。 k=8 时这个放大非常凶 —— EP 的通信量能轻松超过 TP。The min(k, N) term is the key one: a token picks k experts, but if several land on the same GPU only one send is needed. So the amplification is the smaller of k and Nep. At k=8 that amplification is fierce — EP's volume can comfortably exceed TP's.

另一个坑是 All-to-All 怕长尾:它要等最慢的那一对完成。如果路由不均衡,某张卡收到的 token 远多于平均, 整个集合通信就被它拖住。这正是 EPLB(把热门专家复制到多卡)存在的理由。The other trap is that All-to-All is tail-sensitive: it waits for the slowest pair. If routing is unbalanced and one GPU receives far more tokens than average, the whole collective is held up by it. That is exactly why EPLB exists, replicating hot experts across GPUs.

八、CP:Ulysses 与 Ring 的分水岭8 · CP: the watershed between Ulysses and Ring

切序列有两条路线,它们的通信量随并行度的走势完全相反,这是选型的核心依据。There are two routes for sharding the sequence, and their volumes trend in opposite directions with parallelism. That is the core criterion for choosing.

每卡每 micro-batch(整模型)Per GPU per micro-batch (whole model) Ulysses = 2 × (2H + 2Hkv) · B·S·b · (N−1)/N² · L ~ O(1/N)
Ring = 2 × 2 · B·S·Hkv·b · (N−1)/N · L ~ O(1)

Ulysses 的量随 N 递减,Ring 的量随 N 递增然后趋于常数。 原因在原语:Ulysses 用 All-to-All,每卡只发自己那份小分片,N 越大分片越小; Ring 要绕环 N−1 步,步数随 N 涨,抵消了每步块变小的好处。Ulysses falls with N; Ring rises with N and then flattens. The reason is the primitive: Ulysses uses All-to-All, where each GPU sends only its own small shard, and the shard shrinks as N grows; Ring has to go N−1 steps around, and the step count grows with N, cancelling the benefit of smaller blocks.

动图 3:两条路线随并行度的走势(交叉点在 N≈4–8)Figure 3: how the two trend with parallelism (they cross around N≈4–8)
NUlysses(All-to-All)Ring(P2P 环)Ring (P2P ring)
N 小的时候 Ring 更省,N 大的时候 Ulysses 更省,交叉点在 4 到 8 之间。 但 Ulysses 有个硬上限:并行度不能超过 head 数(GQA 下受 KV head 数掐得更紧,比如 Hy3 只有 8)。 所以真正的选型是:head 够分就用 Ulysses,不够分就上 Ring,又长又要大并行就用 USP 把两者叠成 2D。At small N Ring is cheaper; at large N Ulysses is cheaper, crossing somewhere between 4 and 8. But Ulysses has a hard ceiling: parallelism cannot exceed the head count (tighter under GQA, where the KV head count binds — Hy3 has only 8). So the real decision is: enough heads to go around, use Ulysses; not enough, use Ring; long and highly parallel, stack both into 2D with USP.

十、实战:先定排布,再定并行度10 · In practice: fix the layout before the degrees

知道了量级,选型就有了依据。实战里最容易被忽略、也最致命的一步不是"选哪个策略",而是 把哪个策略放进 NVLink 域。同样的 TP=8,放节点内和跨节点能差一个数量级。Once you know the magnitudes, the choice has a basis. The step most often overlooked in practice, and the most damaging, is not "which strategy" but which strategy goes into the NVLink domain. The same TP=8 differs by an order of magnitude inside a node versus across nodes.

排序原则很简单:通信量大、频率高、且是集合通信的往里放。 TP 每层 4 次同步,必须最内;PP 只在边界 P2P 一次,放最外;DP 虽然量大但每 step 才一次,而且能和反向计算重叠,可以往外放。The ordering principle is simple: high volume, high frequency and collective goes innermost. TP synchronises 4 times per layer and must be innermost; PP does one P2P per boundary and goes outermost; DP is large but happens once per step and overlaps the backward pass, so it can sit further out.

十一、最少要几张卡11 · The minimum GPU count

先算显存下界,再找一组能整除的并行度组合。训练和推理的公式完全不同, 因为训练要装优化器状态,推理要装 KV cache。Compute the memory lower bound first, then find a combination of degrees that divides evenly. Training and inference use entirely different formulas, because training has to hold optimizer state and inference has to hold KV cache.

训练(混合精度 + AdamW + ZeRO-3)Training (mixed precision + AdamW + ZeRO-3) 每参数字节 = bf16 权重 2 + bf16 梯度 2 + fp32 master 4 + Adam m 4 + v 4 = 16 B/param
Nmin = ⌈ (16·P + 激活) / (C · u) ⌉bytes/param = bf16 weights 2 + bf16 gradients 2 + fp32 master 4 + Adam m 4 + v 4 = 16 B/param
Nmin = ⌈ (16·P + activations) / (C · u) ⌉
推理Inference Nmin = ⌈ (P·bw + 2·L·Hkv·b·S·并发) / (C · u) ⌉Nmin = ⌈ (P·bw + 2·L·Hkv·b·S·concurrency) / (C · u) ⌉

C 是单卡显存,u 是可用率(框架 workspace、碎片、通信缓冲,一般取 0.85–0.9)。 算出下界之后还要满足整除约束,否则跑不起来:C is per-GPU memory and u the usable fraction (framework workspace, fragmentation, communication buffers — usually 0.85–0.9). Once you have the lower bound, the divisibility constraints still have to hold or it will not run:

十二、要点12 · Takeaways

01
量级差三个数量级Three orders of magnitude separate them

同口径下(每 micro-batch)EP 约 70 GiB、TP 约 17.5 GiB、CP 约 1–2 GiB、PP 每边界只有 64 MiB。选型前先知道自己在哪个量级,比任何调参都重要。On the same basis (per micro-batch), EP is about 70 GiB, TP about 17.5 GiB, CP 1–2 GiB, and PP only 64 MiB per boundary. Knowing which magnitude you are in matters more than any amount of tuning.

07
加 DP 卡不会降低每卡通信量Adding DP ranks does not reduce per-GPU communication

ZeRO-3 的 All-Gather 必须把参数拼回完整一份才能算,所以每卡收发量 ≈ 3Pb,与 DP 度无关。DP 摊薄的是 batch,不是通信。ZeRO-3's All-Gather has to reassemble a full copy of the parameters before computing, so per-GPU traffic is ≈ 3Pb, independent of the DP degree. What DP thins out is the batch, not the communication.

02
Megatron-SP 免费,ZeRO-3 不免费Megatron-SP is free; ZeRO-3 is not

All-Reduce = RS + AG,所以 SP 把它拆开后通信量一字未变,省的激活显存是白拿的。但 ZeRO-3 是 3Pb 对朴素 DP 的 2Pb,多 50%,这是分片存储的真实代价。All-Reduce = RS + AG, so splitting it for SP leaves the volume unchanged and the activation memory saved is free. ZeRO-3, though, is 3Pb against naive DP's 2Pb — 50% more, the real price of sharded storage.

03
只有 TP 与 S 强耦合且每层同步Only TP is both coupled to S and synchronised every layer

TP 通信量正比于 L·B·S·H,80 层就是 320 次集合通信。这决定了它必须在 NVLink 域内,也决定了它是长序列下最先失控的那个。TP's volume is proportional to L·B·S·H, and 80 layers means 320 collectives. That is why it must live inside the NVLink domain, and why it is the first thing to go out of control at long sequence lengths.

04
EP 的放大系数是 min(k, N)EP's amplification factor is min(k, N)

不是 k。多个选中专家落在同一张卡时只发一次。top-k 越大、EP 度越大,All-to-All 越重,而且怕路由长尾 —— 这是 EPLB 的由来。Not k. When several chosen experts land on one GPU, only one send happens. Larger top-k and larger EP degree both make the All-to-All heavier, and it is sensitive to routing tails — hence EPLB.

05
Ulysses 与 Ring 的走势相反Ulysses and Ring trend in opposite directions

Ulysses ~ O(1/N) 递减但被 head 数卡死;Ring ~ O(1) 不随 N 下降但没有上限。交叉点在 N≈4–8,这就是选型分水岭。Ulysses is O(1/N) and falling but capped by the head count; Ring is O(1) and does not fall with N but has no ceiling. They cross at N≈4–8, and that is the watershed.

06
排布比选型更容易出事Layout goes wrong more easily than selection

TP 进 NVLink、PP 走最外层、DP 因为能和反向重叠可以往外放。同样的并行度组合,排布错了性能能差一个数量级,而这一步不会报错。TP into NVLink, PP outermost, and DP further out because it overlaps the backward. The same combination of degrees can differ by an order of magnitude depending on layout — and this step raises no error when you get it wrong.

通信量按 ring 算法的每卡收发字节数估算,忽略延迟项、实现常数与重叠效果,只保留与 B/S/H/L/N 的比例关系, 用于横向比较量级,不等于实测。最小卡数为显存下界,未计通信缓冲与调度余量。Volumes are estimated as per-GPU bytes sent and received under the ring algorithm, ignoring latency terms, implementation constants and overlap, keeping only the proportionality to B/S/H/L/N. They are for comparing magnitudes, not measurements. The minimum GPU count is a memory lower bound and excludes communication buffers and scheduling headroom.
